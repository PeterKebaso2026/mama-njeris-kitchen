About Mama Njeri's Kitchen

Our story

Our journey began in 2015 with a simple passion for home-cooked Kenyan dishes. We always say karibu to every single person who walks through our doors.

From one jiko to two hundred customers

What started small quickly blossomed as word of our food spread. Orders close at 11 am for lunch delivery, so be sure to book early!

Moving to Kenyatta Avenue

To accommodate our family of patrons, we relocated to the heart of the avenue. We believe that good food changes everything about your day.
